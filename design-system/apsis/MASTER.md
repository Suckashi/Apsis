## Product direction

Apsis is a conversation-first workspace for a team of Bots. Every work surface should make four things clear: the current Bot and topic, the current action, whether the user needs to respond, and where to inspect the result. Preserve colorful Bot avatars, projects, shared Skills and permission enforcement. All 48 avatars are directly selectable; do not introduce points, rewards, draws or ownership locks. Keep avatar selection inside Bot customization and apply changes only when the form is saved. Use short summaries with expandable, chronological evidence.

Keep the existing neutral surfaces, Apsis purple and system fonts. Use restrained typography, minimal surfaces, visible focus and purposeful motion. The conversation and its results determine the visual hierarchy.
