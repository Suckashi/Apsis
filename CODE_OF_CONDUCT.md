# Community conduct

Be respectful, specific and constructive when discussing Apsis. People with different experience, backgrounds and accessibility needs should be able to participate.

Harassment, threats, discriminatory insults, sexual harassment, doxxing and publishing another person's private information are not acceptable. Critique proposals and code without attacking people. Respect maintainers' scope decisions and other contributors' time.

Maintainers may edit or remove harmful content, close disruptive discussions, or restrict participation. Decisions should reflect the behavior and its impact, and may be reconsidered when new information is available.

Use GitHub's **Report content** control for abuse or private-information exposure in repository discussions, issues and pull requests. For non-sensitive project moderation questions, contact the maintainer through a repository issue; do not repeat private information or harassment in public reports. Security vulnerabilities follow the separate [security policy](SECURITY.md).
